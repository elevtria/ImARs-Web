Expand one of the three terms, choose a date for each topic, and use the lock icon to prevent edits. Setting a date marks that topic Done.

03 · SCAN AR

Open the camera view

Allow camera access, choose Scan AR, and point the camera at a printed marker. In this build the page opens the camera, but marker recognition and 3D launch are not connected yet.

▦
04 · SCAN QR

Use the QR camera mode

Choose Scan QR and allow camera access. QR recognition and opening a student record are not connected in this build.

IF SOMETHING DOESN’T WORK

Try these quick checks

  • Allow camera access before using either scan mode.
  • For roster work, use the exact template headers and section name.
  • Ask the administrator about missing sections or student imports.
