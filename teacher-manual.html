02 · PREPARE

Plan lessons and prepare activities

Manage lesson access, review quizzes, and download classroom markers.

▦
TOPIC SCHEDULER · DATES

Schedule lesson access

Expand a term and select Set date. A future date keeps the topic locked until release; today makes it available now. Save the change, or clear the date to cancel the schedule.

Term counts and Year Progress show unlocked topics, not student completion.

Open Topic Scheduler ↗
▣
TOPIC SCHEDULER · LOCKS

Control topic availability

Use a topic’s lock button, or Lock All / Unlock All for a term. Manual changes clear the scheduled date; set a new date if you want a later release.

Select your section and wait for “Saved” before leaving.

＋
QUIZ GENERATOR · SETUP

Create questions from a lesson

Upload a PDF, DOCX, or PPTX up to 10 MB. Add a title and section, then choose 1–50 questions, difficulty, and question type. Convert older PowerPoint files to PPTX or PDF first.

Select Generate quiz to create AI suggestions from the material.

Open Quiz Generator ↗
✓
QUIZ GENERATOR · REVIEW

Check every question and answer

Accept useful suggestions and discard unsuitable ones. Edit wording, choices, and correct answers. Add or remove questions as needed, up to 50 total.

Check accuracy and suitability before saving the quiz.

◷
QUIZ GENERATOR · SAVE

Choose when a quiz is available

Choose Save as draft, Available now, or Schedule for a future date and time. Scheduled times use your device’s local time.

Save the quiz and confirm it appears in My quizzes. Use Edit or Delete as needed.

↓
OFFLINE SYNC & AR MARKERS

Prepare marker files before class

Download a topic ZIP or all markers, then extract the images for the student AR app. AR activities run in that app.

Progress tracks requests, so confirm the ZIP downloaded. Export Report saves marker download status, not grades.

03 · SYNC & REVIEW

Sync offline results with a Grade QR code

Use the student’s results QR code, then review the saved records.

QR offline sync onlyThe portal scanner transfers student results. It does not scan AR markers or open 3D lessons. Keep the teacher device online to save results.
1
SCAN OR UPLOAD

Read the student’s Grade QR

Ask the student to display their Grade QR. Open Scan QR (Offline Sync), allow camera access, and keep the whole code inside the frame.

You can also upload a clear screenshot or image of the code.

Open QR offline sync ↗
2
CHECK THE RESULT

Confirm the student and sync status

Check the student’s name, ID, averages, and lesson breakdown. Wait for “All Terms & Lessons Synced!” to confirm the save.

Recent Scans lists QR activity. Reopen Student Records or Analytics for updated results.

3
RETRY OR CONTINUE

Finish one student at a time

If the dialog says “Displayed from QR (Offline Mode),” saving was not confirmed. Reconnect, scan or upload again, and wait for success.

Choose Scan Another for the next student. Reopen the camera if needed.

▤
STUDENT RECORDS

Review each learner

Search by name or filter by section. Records show AR and module time, test results, overall score, and last activity.

Missing time means it was not recorded. Grade QR sync transfers test results, not AR or module time.

Open Student Records ↗
↗
ANALYTICS

Compare progress across lessons

Compare pre/post-test averages and improvement. Search or filter the student table, and switch terms for topic completion rates.

Table filters affect student rows; the overview remains a combined summary.

Open Analytics ↗

QUICK CHECKS

Resolve common problems

  • Camera blocked: allow camera access, or upload a QR image.
  • QR unreadable: improve lighting, keep the whole code visible, or crop closer without cutting its edges.
  • Missing data: check your account, section, connection, and sync confirmation.
  • Still stuck: contact your school administrator with the page name and error message.
