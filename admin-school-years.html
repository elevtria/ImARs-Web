School Year & History

Close a year with its records preserved, then import the next year’s student roster.

Current school year

What happens when you close this year?

  1. Save a read-only history of the roster, section assignments, tests, quizzes, submissions, activity results, and recorded learning time.
  2. Archive student accounts and clear the live results, generated quizzes, and release schedules. Teacher accounts and lesson content stay.
  3. Open the next school year. Import a new student roster to begin.

Closing is a school-wide action. Verify the current-year reports and finish grading first. The archive uses records saved in Supabase. The archive and reset are saved together; a rejected database transaction changes neither. Check the year carefully. Archived history remains downloadable; reopening a closed year is not supported from this page.
